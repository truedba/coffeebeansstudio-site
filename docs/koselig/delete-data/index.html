Delete your data

Koselig by Coffee Beans Studio

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Koselig has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. We do not keep your progress on our servers.

Delete your data in the game

Open Settings, tap Delete my data at the bottom and confirm. The game will:

  • erase progress, save slots and settings from your phone; only the record of the “No ads” purchase (without payment details), a note that the deletion was done and technical settings of the SDKs on the device remain;
  • delete Koselig’s copy in the cloud: on Android, Koselig’s saved games in Google Play Games are deleted and the game checks that none are left; on iPhone, the deletion is sent to your iCloud account and Apple applies it at its next sync, so for at least 24 hours and 3 launches with iCloud available the game neither uploads nor downloads progress;
  • turn off statistics (analytics) and crash reports — they stay off until you reinstall the game or turn Statistics on again — erase analytics data stored on the phone, delete unsent crash reports and delete the Firebase installation identifier, also on Google’s servers (without internet, this is retried at the next launch); at the next launch, Remote Config creates a new identifier that is not linked to the old one;
  • show an installation code. Send it to support [at] coffeebeansstudio [dot] eu, and we will delete the statistics already sent from this installation right away; we answer within one month.

If you do not send the code, statistics already sent are deleted when the 2-month retention period ends; Google removes expired data once a month, so in practice this takes up to about 3 months. Crash reports are deleted after 90 days. Aggregated reports that cannot identify you remain.

No internet, not signed in or iCloud turned off: the phone is cleared at once; the cloud copy is deleted at the next launch with internet and sign-in, and until then the game neither uploads nor downloads progress.

Another device with the same Apple ID or Google account: delete your data there too, otherwise it may upload its progress to the cloud again.

The “No ads” purchase is not affected — you can restore it with Restore. Google ads follow your choice in the consent form (Settings → Ads & data, where shown). Your Play Games profile and your Game Center data are managed by Google and Apple — see below.

Data on your device

Progress, saves, settings, the “No ads” purchase status and your ad consent choice are stored on your phone. To delete all of it, delete the app:

  • iPhone: touch and hold the Koselig icon → Remove App → Delete App.
  • Android: touch and hold the Koselig icon → App info → Uninstall (or Storage → Clear storage to keep the app but erase its data).

Deleting the app does not cancel or remove the “No ads” purchase — it belongs to your Apple ID or Google account and can be restored later.

iCloud copy and Game Center (iPhone)

On iPhone, progress and save slots are also copied to your own iCloud account. It is stored by Apple under your Apple ID. To stop it, turn off iCloud for Koselig in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

At launch, the game signs in to Game Center (Apple), which it needs for iCloud saves. Your Game Center profile is managed by Apple: you can turn Game Center off in Settings → Game Center and manage your Apple data at privacy.apple.com.

Google Play Games copy (Android)

On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, progress and save slots are also copied to Saved Games in your Google account. This copy is stored by Google; we cannot read it or delete it for you. You can:

  • delete Koselig’s data in your Play Games profile, where you can remove the data of a single game: play.google.com/games/profile;
  • manage or delete your data in your Google Account;
  • stop the copying: sign out of Google Play Games on your phone, or do not sign in — progress then stays only on the phone.

If the game is still installed and you are still signed in, your progress will be copied again the next time you play.

Analytics and crash reports

Analytics events and crash reports (Google Firebase) are not linked to your name or email, so without the installation code from Delete my data we cannot find one person’s records. With the code, we delete statistics already sent right away; without it, analytics events are deleted after the 2-month retention period (in practice up to about 3 months) and crash reports after 90 days.

Advertising data

Ads in Koselig are provided by Google AdMob. Data collected by Google is controlled by Google. You can:

  • reset or delete your Android Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads;
  • change your consent in the game: Settings → Ads & data;
  • manage or delete data in your Google account: My Ad Center and Google Data & privacy;
  • read how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com.

Emails to us

If you wrote to us and want your messages deleted, send a request to support [at] coffeebeansstudio [dot] eu from the same address. We will delete them within 30 days and confirm.
