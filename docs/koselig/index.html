Koselig

A cozy wooden tray puzzle. Fit every piece into the tray — and take your time.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Puzzle, unhurried

Koselig is a digital wooden tray puzzle. Pieces lie on the table around the tray; you pick them up, turn them and fit them in until every cell is filled.

The levels lead you along a map, from small trays to the full one. There is more than one way to fill a tray — and a perfect solution, where no two pieces of the same colour touch.

When you are not sure a position can still work, tap Stuck?. The bonsai checks it for you after a short video you choose to watch — or straight away if you have bought No ads.

What is inside

  • Three tables

    Oak, linen and walnut — each with its own quiet life: a candle, steam over tea, lavender in the breeze.

  • Trays and pieces

    Trays in pine, walnut, stone, steel, brass, olive and cherry. Pieces in paint, wood, stone, metal and terrazzo.

  • A map of levels

    Gentle first steps on smaller trays, then the full puzzle.

  • The bonsai

    Your helper for the Stuck? check — and it blossoms when you solve a level.

  • Saves

    Three save slots for positions you want to come back to.

  • Seven languages

    English, Español, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Polski and Русский.

Fair about ads

No banners and no pop-ups. A short video plays only when you ask for a Stuck? check and choose to watch it. One optional purchase, No ads, lets the check work without videos.
