Meet Koselig

Brewed slowly

Coffee Beans Studio is a small independent studio. We care about calm puzzles, warm materials and honest design: things you can touch with your eyes — wood, linen, candlelight — and rules you can learn in a minute.

Koselig

A wooden tray puzzle

Fit every piece into the wooden tray. Change the table, the tray and the pieces to suit your mood: oak or linen, walnut or brass, paint or stone. When you are stuck, a small bonsai can tell you whether the position can still be solved.
