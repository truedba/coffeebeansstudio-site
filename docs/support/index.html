Questions

How do I restore the “No ads” purchase?

Tap ☰ in the top right corner to open Settings, then tap Restore next to No ads. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. The purchase is checked with the store every time the game starts, so it also comes back by itself after a reinstall once the store answers.

How do I start a level over?

Open Settings and tap Restart level, then tap Sure? Tap again to confirm. This resets the current level. To erase everything — all progress and settings — delete the app.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. Koselig speaks English, Español, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Polski and Русский. System follows the language of your phone.

When does the game show ads?

Only when you tap Stuck? and choose Watch video. There are no banners and no pop-up ads. With No ads the check works straight away.

How do I change my ad consent?

In Settings, tap Change next to Ads & data — Google’s consent form opens again. The item appears in regions where consent rules apply.

Can I keep a position and come back to it?

Yes. Settings → Saves has three slots: Save stores the current position, Load brings it back.

Deleting your data

Koselig has no accounts. Progress is kept on your device and, on iPhone, in your own iCloud account; on Android, in Google Play Games if you are signed in. To delete it, delete the app and, on Android, Koselig’s data in your Play Games profile; Settings → Delete my data in the game opens the data deletion page with all the steps, including iCloud. To stop statistics, turn off Statistics in Settings. Next to the Statistics switch, Reset my statistics erases the statistics and unsent crash reports kept on your device, and new statistics are no longer tied to what was sent before. It does not delete what has already been sent — to have that deleted, write to us.
