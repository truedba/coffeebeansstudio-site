Privacy Policy

Koselig and coffeebeansstudio.eu

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you play Koselig on iPhone or Android and when you visit coffeebeansstudio.eu. We wrote it to match what the app actually does.

Who we are

Koselig is published by Coffee Beans Studio. The controller of your personal data under the GDPR is Dzmitry Kurtsikau, a private individual based in Poland (“we”, “us”). Postal address: Złota 83/90, 00-819 Warszawa, Poland. For any question about privacy, write to support [at] coffeebeansstudio [dot] eu.

In short

  • There are no Koselig accounts and no sign-in to us. For cloud saves only, at launch the game signs in to Game Center on iPhone and asks you to sign in to Google Play Games on Android. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device; on iPhone they are also copied to your own iCloud account, and on Android to your own Google Play Games account if you are signed in to it. We do not receive them.
  • The game shows ads only when you choose to watch a short rewarded video for the Stuck? check. There are no banners and no interstitial ads. The ads come from Google AdMob, which processes device data and identifiers.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent through Google’s consent form before personalised ads are shown.
  • The optional “No ads” purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • Koselig uses Google Firebase for game statistics and crash reports. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland they are on only with your consent; elsewhere you can turn them off. The separate Statistics switch in Settings works everywhere, and your choice there comes first. Statistics do not include your name, email or contacts. We do not sell personal data.

1. Data stored on your device

Koselig saves the following on your phone so you can continue where you left off:

  • game progress: opened levels, the current position of the pieces and your three save slots;
  • settings: sound, language, the chosen table, tray and pieces;
  • whether you own the “No ads” purchase;
  • your advertising consent choice, which is stored on the device by Google’s consent SDK.

This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the app. On iPhone, progress and save slots are also copied to your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage), so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling. This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple ID; we cannot read it. You can stop it in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud. At launch, the game signs in to Game Center (Apple), which it needs for iCloud saves; Apple receives your Game Center player ID and the fact that you play Koselig. You can turn Game Center off in iOS Settings → Game Center. The “No ads” purchase can always be restored from Apple or Google (Settings → Restore).

On Android, the game asks you to sign in to Google Play Games at launch. If you are signed in, progress and save slots are also copied to your Google account (Saved Games in Google Play Games Services), so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling. Koselig has no accounts of its own. This copy is stored by Google in your account; we cannot read it, and it does not reach our servers. Through the Play Games SDK, Google receives your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) and the saved game data, and also collects analytics and diagnostic data on how its SDK works. See the Google Privacy Policy. If you do not sign in to Google Play Games, or sign out of it, progress stays only on your phone.

2. Advertising: Google AdMob

Koselig uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show rewarded videos. To have a video ready when you ask for one, the app starts the SDK at launch and loads one video in the background. This means Google may receive data from your device even if you never watch a video.

Data that Google may collect and process through the SDK includes:

  • device identifiers: on Android the Advertising ID; on iPhone the identifier for vendors and other device signals;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate an approximate location (country or city);
  • device and app information: model, operating system and version, language, screen size, app version;
  • interactions with ads: which ads were loaded, shown, watched to the end or tapped.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse and — only with your consent where consent is required — to personalise ads. Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which still use some device data for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

On iPhone, Koselig asks once for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) before ads are loaded. If you allow it, Google may use the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalise and measure ads. If you refuse, the IDFA is not available to the app or to Google, and the game works the same. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK declares the Android Privacy Sandbox permissions for the Topics and Attribution Reporting APIs and for the Privacy Sandbox advertising ID. Where your phone supports them, Google may use them to show and measure ads; you can turn them off in your phone’s ad privacy settings.

Other ad SDKs (Android). We show ads from Google AdMob only; ad mediation with other networks is turned off in our AdMob account. The Android version nevertheless contains the SDKs of Meta Audience Network and AppLovin, which AdMob can use for mediation. They do not show ads in Koselig, but when the app starts they may contact their own servers and receive technical data about your device. See the Meta Privacy Policy and the AppLovin Privacy Policy. If we turn on mediation with another network, we will update this policy first.

3. Your consent and how to change it

In regions where consent is required, Koselig shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can accept, refuse or choose purposes. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ads & data (this item appears where the consent rules apply). Right below it, the separate Statistics item, shown to everyone, turns game statistics and crash reports on or off (see section 4). Deleting and reinstalling the app also resets your choice.

You can also limit ads on your device: on Android in Settings → Google → Ads (reset or delete the Advertising ID); on iPhone in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

4. Game analytics and crash reports: Google Firebase

Koselig uses Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config (Google). We use them to see which levels are too hard, to find and fix crashes and to adjust game settings without an app update.

When it is on. Where the GDPR consent rules apply (the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), analytics is turned on only if you agree in Google’s consent form to IAB TCF purposes 1, 7, 8, 9 and 10 (storing information on your device, measuring ad and content performance, understanding audiences, improving services), and crash reports only with purposes 1 and 10. Until you answer the form, nothing is collected. Elsewhere they are on by default on the basis of our legitimate interest (section 8). Everywhere, the Statistics switch in Settings turns them on or off, and your choice there takes precedence over the consent form.

  • Analytics receives an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase and events about how the game is used: levels started, finished or reset, hints and the Stuck? check, settings opened, language changed, the “No ads” purchase started, completed or failed. We do not link it to your name, email or advertising ID.
  • Crashlytics, after a crash, receives a crash report: the technical state of the app, the stack trace, device model and OS version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier.
  • Remote Config downloads game settings and sends your country, language, time zone, app and OS version.

Google processes this data on our behalf. Analytics event data is kept for no longer than 2 months, crash reports for 90 days. See Privacy and security in Firebase.

5. The “No ads” purchase

Koselig offers one optional in-app purchase, “No ads” (product koselig.no_ads). It is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your name, card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from the store that the purchase is owned and stores that fact on your device. See the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. What we do not do

  • No Koselig accounts, no sign-in to us, no social features; Game Center on iPhone and Google Play Games on Android are used only for cloud saves.
  • No tracking by us across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for anything except what is described here.

The app uses internet access for ads, statistics and crash reports, the purchase and cloud saves (iCloud and Game Center on iPhone, Google Play Games on Android). On Android it declares only these permissions: internet and network state; vibration for haptic feedback; Google Play purchases and licence check; the advertising ID (Google’s and the Privacy Sandbox one). The rest are technical permissions of the SDKs: background tasks (WorkManager: wake lock and foreground service), basic phone state and the install referrer for Google Play and ads. Koselig does not send notifications.

If the game asks you to rate it, it uses Google Play In-App Review or Apple’s StoreKit rating prompt. The rating window is shown and handled by Google or Apple; we do not learn whether or how you rated the game.

7. Children

Koselig is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not intended for children under 16 — in Poland, 16 is the age from which you can consent to the processing of your data yourself — and it is not part of a children’s category. Ads in Koselig are not configured as child-directed. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — personalised advertising and storing or accessing information on your device where consent is required; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, also game statistics (analytics) and crash reports.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — showing non-personalised ads to fund a free game, ad measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, game statistics and crash reports to fix and improve the game. You can object at any time with the Statistics switch.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — delivering and restoring the “No ads” purchase.

Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through its ads SDK. Google Play Games (Android) and Game Center (iPhone) are services of Google and Apple; they process your player profile and saved games under their own privacy policies. Google and Apple may process data outside the European Economic Area; these transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and on Google’s and Apple’s standard contractual clauses.

9. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays there until you delete the app. The iCloud copy of your progress stays in your iCloud account. The Google Play Games copy stays in your Google account until you delete it. Analytics event data is kept for no longer than 2 months and crash reports for 90 days. Data linked to your device (the app-instance identifier) is also kept for no longer than 2 months, and this period is not extended when you play again. If you use Delete my data and do not send us the code (section 10), statistics already sent are deleted when the 2-month retention period ends; Google removes expired data once a month, so in practice this takes up to about 3 months. Aggregated reports that cannot identify anyone are kept. Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google’s policies. If you write to us, we keep your email only as long as needed to answer and solve your request.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. We do not hold game data on our servers. To delete your data, use Settings → Delete my data in the game: it erases your data on the phone and the cloud copy and turns statistics off (details on the data deletion page). Statistics already sent to Google Firebase cannot be linked to you without the code shown at the end of the deletion (“Code to request immediate deletion of statistics already sent”, with a Copy button); send us this code, and we will ask Google to delete those statistics right away. After deletion, only the record of the “No ads” purchase (without payment details), a note that the deletion was done and technical settings of the SDKs remain on the phone; statistics stay off until you turn Statistics back on yourself. Requests about advertising data can be made to Google through the links above. You can always write to us at support [at] coffeebeansstudio [dot] eu; we answer within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) — or with the authority in your country.

11. This website

coffeebeansstudio.eu is a static website. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and its domain name service is provided by Cloudflare. These providers may process your IP address and technical request data in server logs for security and delivery. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

12. Changes

If the app starts doing something new with data — for example a new ad network or analytics — we will update this page before the change reaches the app, and change the date below.
